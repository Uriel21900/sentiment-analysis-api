# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

- **Be respectful**: Be respectful of differing opinions, experiences, and knowledge levels.
- **Be welcoming**: Create a space for a diverse and inclusive environment.
- **Be constructive**: Provide constructive criticism and welcome it gracefully.
- **Be empathetic**: Show empathy towards other community members.

## What Not to Do

- No sexist, racist, ableist, or otherwise discriminatory jokes or language.
- No trolling, insulting comments, or public/private harassment.
- No publishing private information without explicit permission.
- No promoting or supporting oppressive systems.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community moderators are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate action against unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and it also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces.
